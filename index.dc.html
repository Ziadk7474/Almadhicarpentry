New to pallets?

Wooden pallets, quickly

Why wooden pallets?

Cheaper than steel or plastic, strong enough for heavy freight, and fully recyclable once retired.

How are they made?

Timber is sourced and checked, cut to spec, nailed together on the bench, then heat-treated to ISPM-15.

What are they used for?

Shipping, warehousing and export logistics — anywhere goods move in bulk on a truck or in a container.

Are they reusable?

Yes — inspected, repaired and put back into service across many load cycles before retirement.

From timber to pallet

How it’s made

Four steps, one yard — nothing here is subcontracted out.

Step 01

Raw timber

Every consignment is checked for moisture and rot before it is stacked — wet or warped stock never reaches the saw.

Step 02

Cut & jointed

Boards are cut to spec and nailed to a fixed pattern per grade — heavy duty decks get the reinforced pattern without being asked.

Step 03

Assembled

Runners, blocks and deck boards come together on the bench, checked against the sample before the batch is released.

Step 04

Stacked & ready

Stock sizes wait in the yard for same or next-day collection; custom runs get a completion date with the quote.

Before you order

Common questions

What pallet sizes can you make?

Whatever size you need. The most common size shipped is 100 × 120 cm, and odd sizes are built to your measurements — send a drawing, a photo or the pallet you use now.

How much weight will a pallet hold?

It depends entirely on the build. Tell us the weight and what it is carrying and we will spec the boards and base to suit — that is why we quote rather than publish a table.

Do you sell used pallets?

Yes. Used pallets are checked for broken boards and loose blocks before leaving the yard, and are sold by the piece or as a mixed bulk lot.

What are the wood shavings made from?

The wood we cut for pallets. Nothing painted or chemically finished goes into the shavings line, and they come chip shaped rather than pressed.

How soon can you deliver?

Standard sizes held in stock are usually ready the same or next day. Custom orders depend on quantity, and a completion date is given with the quote.

Is there a minimum order?

There is no fixed minimum. Small orders are accepted, and the price per pallet or per sack improves with larger quantities.
