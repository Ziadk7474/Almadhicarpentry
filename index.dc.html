Before you order

Common questions

What pallet sizes can you make?

Whatever size you need. The most common size shipped is 100 × 120 cm, and odd sizes are built to your measurements — send a drawing, a photo or the pallet you use now.

How much weight will a pallet hold?

It depends entirely on the build. Tell us the weight and what it is carrying and we will spec the boards and base to suit — that is why we quote rather than publish a table.

Do you sell used pallets?

Yes. Used pallets are checked for broken boards and loose blocks before leaving the yard, and are sold by the piece or as a mixed bulk lot.

What are the wood shavings made from?

The wood we cut for pallets. Nothing painted or chemically finished goes into the shavings line, and they come chip shaped rather than pressed.

How soon can you deliver?

Standard sizes held in stock are usually ready the same or next day. Custom orders depend on quantity, and a completion date is given with the quote.

Is there a minimum order?

There is no fixed minimum. Small orders are accepted, and the price per pallet or per sack improves with larger quantities.
