Custom sized pallets

Any size. Any shape you need.

Most of what leaves this yard is made to order, so a size you cannot find elsewhere is usually still a size we can build. Send a drawing, a photo, a sample pallet, or just the dimensions of your carton — no fixed minimum.

How to give us a size
Minimum order None A single custom pallet is quoted the same as a full run.
Three ways to spec it

However you have the size

Pick whichever is easiest — none of them need to be exact drawings.

Option 01

Send a drawing

A CAD file, a sketch, or a spec sheet from your carton supplier — whatever you already have.

Option 02

Send a photo

A photo with a tape measure in shot of the pallet or space you are matching against.

Option 03

Send a sample

Drop off the pallet you use now and we will match it board for board.

Sizes & specification

We build to the size you give us

Board count, board thickness, spacing and base height all change with the job, so we do not publish a fixed specification — we quote against your measurement or your sample. Unusually large, unusually small, non-standard aspect ratios: all normal requests here.

Custom sizing questions

Worth knowing

How exact does my measurement need to be?

As close as you can get it. If you are unsure, send the range and we will confirm the final dimension with you before the batch goes to the saw.

What is the smallest or largest you will build?

There is no hard limit at either end. Tell us the dimension and we will confirm whether it needs a different board pattern to hold together.

How long does a custom run take?

You get a completion date with the quote once the sample is approved. Large runs are staged so part of the order can ship early if you need it.

Can I combine a custom size with a specific wood grade?

Yes — see custom quality pallets and mention both in the same enquiry.
