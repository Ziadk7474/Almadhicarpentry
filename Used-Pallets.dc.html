Used pallet questions

Worth knowing

Are used pallets structurally safe?

Everything sold as Good grade or Economy has been checked for broken boards and loose blocks. If a pallet does not pass, it does not go out at that grade.

Can I mix sizes in a bulk lot?

Bulk lots are assorted by nature — mostly 100 × 120 cm with some variation. Tell us if you need a tighter size range and we will check what is available.

Do you collect old pallets too?

Yes — see our We Buy page if you have pallets, timber or offcuts to sell rather than buy.

Is there a minimum order?

No fixed minimum. Single pieces are fine, and the price improves with quantity.
