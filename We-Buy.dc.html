Selling to us — questions

Worth knowing

Is there a minimum quantity?

No fixed minimum, though pricing per piece improves with volume. A single pallet or a full truckload are both worth a call.

How is the price decided?

Condition, wood type and volume. A photo is usually enough for a rough number; a site visit confirms it for larger lots.

Do you collect, or do I need to deliver?

We collect larger lots on our own transport, usually the same day. Smaller quantities can be dropped at the Al Sajaa yard if that is easier for you.

What areas do you collect from?

All seven emirates, same as our delivery network. Larger lots further out are still worth a call — ask and we will confirm.

How is payment made?

Cash in hand on pickup for most lots. Ask about other arrangements for large, recurring volumes.
