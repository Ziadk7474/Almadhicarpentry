How we work

From timber to your truck.

Four steps, one yard. Nothing is sent out to anyone else.

  1. 01

    Raw timber

    Every load is checked for moisture and rot. Wet or warped wood never reaches the saw.

  2. 02

    Cut and nailed

    Boards are cut to size and nailed in a set pattern for each grade. Heavy duty gets the stronger pattern as standard.

  3. 03

    Checked and stacked

    Each batch is checked against the sample before it is released. Stock sizes wait in the yard for pickup.

  4. 04

    Delivered

    Our own trucks cover all seven emirates. Collecting from Al Sajaa costs less.
