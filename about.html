Who we are

Built on trust, delivered on time.

Al Madhi Carpentry started in 2003 in the Al Sajaa Industrial Area, Sharjah. For twenty years the work was pallets and nothing else: new pallets built from wood, old and used pallets, and used wood from pallets we take apart. In October 2025 we added wood shavings from the same saws.

We have never been a trading company. Every pallet is cut, nailed and stacked by our own people in our own yard. That is why we can give a real finish date and stand behind it.

Our mission

Make the right pallet for the load, at a price the customer can repeat next month.

Our vision

A yard where nothing is wasted. Old pallets become boards, boards become pallets, and what the saw takes off becomes bedding.

Message from the founder

Every pallet carries our name.

When I started Al Madhi Carpentry in 2003, it was a small yard with one rule: every pallet had to be one our customers could rely on.

More than twenty years later, that rule has not changed. Whether it is one custom pallet or a large shipment, it leaves our yard with our name on it. I am grateful to every customer, supplier and member of our team who has been part of this.

Bilal AhmedFounder, Al Madhi Carpentry

Quality

How we keep the standard.

01

Wood intake

Every load is checked for moisture and rot before it is stacked. Wet or warped wood goes back to the supplier, not onto the saw.

02

Nailing and assembly

Set nail patterns for each grade, checked against the sample pallet on the bench.

03

Load check

The first pallet of every custom run is loaded and checked before the batch is released. If it bends, the build changes first.

04

Heat treatment on request

ISPM-15 treated and marked wood for export. Tell us the destination country with your enquiry.
