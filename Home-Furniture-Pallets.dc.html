Home & furniture questions

Worth knowing

Is the wood safe to use indoors?

Yes — ask for untreated, sanded stock and nothing chemical goes near it. See also our custom quality pallets if you need it certified food-safe or export grade too.

Can you build a non-standard size for a specific piece of furniture?

Yes, send your project dimensions the same way you would for custom sized pallets and we will build to match.

Do you deliver a single pallet?

Yes, small orders are collected from the yard or added to the next delivery run in your area.

Can I choose the wood tone or grain?

Within what the yard has in stock, yes. Tell us the look you are after and we will pick boards to suit.
