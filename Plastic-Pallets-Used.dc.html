Used plastic questions

Worth knowing

How much cheaper is used versus new?

It depends on condition and model, but expect a meaningful saving over new stock. Ask for both prices side by side.

Is used plastic still food-grade cleanable?

Yes, the material itself does not degrade the way wood does. It still hoses down and cleans the same as new.

Can I sell my used plastic pallets to you?

Speak to us directly about plastic buy-back — our standard We Buy page covers wood, but ask and we will look at plastic too.
