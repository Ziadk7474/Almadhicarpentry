Common questions

Before you order

What sizes can you make?

Anything between 60 and 200 cm in either direction. The size we ship most is 100 × 120 cm. If your carton is an odd size, send the measurements and we build the deck around it.

How much weight will a pallet hold?

Light duty is built for about 500 kg, and heavy duty grades reach roughly 2,500 kg depending on the base and board thickness. Tell us what the pallet has to carry and we match the grade to it.

Do you sell used pallets?

Yes. Used pallets are checked for broken boards and loose blocks before they leave the yard. You can buy them by the piece or as a mixed bulk lot.

How soon can I get them?

Standard sizes held in stock are usually ready the same day or the next day. Custom orders depend on the quantity, and we give you a completion date with the quote.

Do you deliver outside Sharjah?

Yes, across the UAE. Delivery is charged on distance and load size. If you have your own transport, collection from the Al Sajaa yard is cheaper.

Is there a minimum order?

No fixed minimum. Small orders are fine, though the price per pallet improves as the quantity goes up.

Can I use these for export?

Tell us the destination country when you enquire. Export packaging rules differ by country, so we confirm what your shipment needs before you place the order.

Send us the size. We’ll price it.

Dimensions, base type and load weight are enough to start. If it is easier, call the yard and tell us on the phone.
