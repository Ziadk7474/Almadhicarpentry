01 · Who we are

Built on trust,
delivered on time

Al Madhi Carpentry started in 2003 in the Al Sajaa Industrial Area, Sharjah. For twenty years the work was pallets and nothing else — every grade, every size, every quality: new pallets built from wood, old and used pallets, and used wood recovered from pallets we take apart. In October 2025 we added wood shavings off the same saws.

We have never been a trading company. Every pallet on this site is cut, nailed and stacked by our own people in our own yard, which is why we can quote a real completion date and stand behind it. What we cannot rebuild, we recycle — old pallets come in, boards come out, and the offcuts go to the shavings line.

Our mission

Make the right pallet for the load in front of us, at a price the customer can repeat next month.

Our vision

A yard where nothing is wasted — the old pallet becomes boards, the boards become a pallet, and what the saw takes off becomes bedding.

04 · Quality standards

Wood intake

Every consignment is checked for moisture and rot before it is stacked. Wet or warped stock goes back to the supplier, not onto the saw.

Nailing & assembly

Fixed nail patterns per grade, checked against the sample pallet on the bench. Heavy duty decks get the reinforced pattern without being asked.

Load check

First-off from every custom run is loaded and inspected before the batch is released. If it flexes, the spec changes before the rest are built.

Heat treatment on request

Export consignments needing ISPM 15 treated and marked wood are arranged on request — tell us the destination country with the enquiry.
