New pallet questions

Worth knowing

What wood do you use?

Softwood pine sourced and checked before it reaches the saw. Wet or warped stock is rejected before it is stacked.

Can you heat treat for export?

Yes, ISPM-15 heat treatment is available on request. Tell us the destination country when you order and we will confirm the stamp and certificate.

How long does a new build take?

Stock sizes usually ship same or next day. Custom runs get a completion date with the quote, and large orders can be staged so part ships early.

Is there a minimum order?

No fixed minimum. Small orders are accepted, and the price per pallet improves with quantity.
